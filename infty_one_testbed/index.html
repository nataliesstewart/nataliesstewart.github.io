Fall 2024

This semester, Babytop will be about the Kervaire invariant one problem and stable equivariant homotopy theory, via HHR. We will aim to cover foundational details using the technology of \(\infty\)-categories and the spectral Mackey functor theorem.

We meet at 4:00 on Tuesdays in location unless otherwise noted.
